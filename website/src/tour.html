A look around Enveliq

These are real screens from Enveliq, filled with made-up example mail. Nothing here is anyone's real email.

Parcels, in one place

Each order gets one card that moves from Ordered to Shipped, Out for delivery and Delivered. You never have to dig through emails to find where a parcel is.

One question when it matters

When a parcel should have arrived, a small card on the home page asks whether it did. One tap answers it, and the card goes away.

Writing help that stays in its box

Press one button and the AI suggests a reply. It appears in its own box, labelled as written by AI, and nothing is sent until you press Send.

New email, with people you know

Start typing a name and Enveliq suggests people from your own mail. Attach files, add Cc or Bcc, and it is sent from the mailbox you pick.

A calm warning, in plain words

When an email looks like a scam, such as a fake delivery notice, it gets a small Be careful label and a note that says exactly what looks odd. Nothing is blocked and you are not told what to think: you decide.

The checks are plain code that compares what an email says with what it does. The AI is not involved. How the Be careful flag works

Is this really from them?

In View original, a plain line says whether your email provider could confirm the email came from that address. If replies would go to a different website, it says so too.

When your provider gives no answer, Enveliq says nothing. It reads your own provider's result and never trusts a line the sender wrote. How it works

A safe way to read the original

When you want the full email, Enveliq fetches it from your mailbox and shows it in a plain, safe view. Scripts are removed, pictures from the internet are not loaded, and links in the text are not clickable. The full email is not saved.

Calm on a phone too

Enveliq works in your phone's browser, with buttons big enough to tap easily, and notes such as the Be careful flag are kept short so the screen never feels crowded. Open it from anywhere with a private connection, without putting it on the open internet.
