# Checks before sending

When you press **Send** on a **New email**, Enveliq looks once at what you wrote, in your browser, and asks a calm question if something looks missed. It never stops you: you can always send.

## What it checks

- **No subject.** "This email has no subject."
- **A forgotten attachment.** If your message says something is attached (words like attach, attached, attachment, enclosed) and no file is attached: "You mention an attachment, but nothing is attached." Lines that start with `>` (quoted text) are ignored.

## What you see

A small window called **Before you send**, with the reason and two buttons:

- **Go back** is the one that is ready when the window opens. It returns you to your email, with the cursor in the subject box. Pressing Escape does the same.
- **Send anyway** sends the email as it is.

Nothing has been sent while the window is open.

## Good to know

- The check is done on the page, so nothing about your email is sent anywhere for it, and it works the same for every mailbox.
- Replies are not checked. A reply takes its subject from the email you are answering, and replies cannot carry attachments.
- It only looks for English words. If you write in another language, it will not notice a forgotten attachment.
- There is no setting to turn it off, because it only ever asks once and you can always choose Send anyway.
