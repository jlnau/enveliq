# Settings and Administration

Settings is for what each person chooses for themselves. Administration is for the person who runs Enveliq. Both open from the menu at the top right, and both are short lists of plain pages.

## Settings (everyone): seven pages

| Page | What is on it |
| --- | --- |
| **Account** | Your profile, then sign-in and security (password, two-factor, passkeys, devices). |
| **Mailboxes** | One card for each mailbox, with **Sync now** and **Edit**, and **Add a mailbox**. |
| **People & writing** | Address book, Important people, Signature and saved replies, and your own AI instructions. |
| **Rules & calendar** | Mail rules, and Calendar and to-do. |
| **Alerts** | Notifications on your devices, and Email alerts. |
| **General** | Undo send, the Reviewed and Archived lists, your Features, Fast search, Your data, and Keyboard shortcuts. |
| **Help** | The setup guide. |

## Administration (administrators only): six pages

| Page | What is on it |
| --- | --- |
| **People & sign-in** | The people list, and the sign-in rules and single sign-on. |
| **Mail** | Automatic sync, Reviewed and Archived limits, Forward to check, and Microsoft sign-in. |
| **AI** | The AI model, the extra summary instructions and Agent chat. |
| **Features** | A two-column checklist of everything that can be switched on or off for everyone. |
| **Notifications** | Reminders and where alerts can be sent. |
| **System** | Backup and restore, Updates, Reporting, and Security and privacy. |

Nothing was removed. Each older page is now a section on the page where you would look for it, and links and bookmarks to the older names (for example `#/settings/calendar`) still open the right page.

## Saving

There are no Save buttons for simple choices. A tick box or a choice saves the moment you change it and shows a quiet **Saved ✓**; a number or a name saves when you leave the box. Buttons are only for actions such as **Sync now**, **Check connection**, **Add** and **Remove**. Two places keep one button on purpose, because saving means making a connection and testing it first: the AI model ("Test and save") and the notification channels ("Save and send a test").

## Mailboxes

Each mailbox is a small card: its coloured initial, its name and address, how it is doing, and two buttons. **Edit** opens that mailbox's own page:

- **Check for new mail automatically**, **Write AI summaries** and **Follow sent mail for replies** (Waiting for reply). All three are ticked for a new mailbox.
- The name and the label colour.
- **Connection**, with **Diagnostics** folded away behind a "Show" fold.
- **Remove this mailbox**, which asks first. Your email itself is never touched.

## Layout

Sections sit side by side when there is room, so most pages need little or no scrolling. On a phone they stack in one column, the buttons are at least 44 pixels tall, and Settings opens full screen.
