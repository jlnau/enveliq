# Settings and Administration

Settings is for what each person chooses for themselves. Administration is for the person who runs Enveliq. Both open from one place: Settings in the account menu at the top right. Settings takes over the whole screen and the left panel becomes its menu, with your own pages above a line and, for administrators only, the Administration pages below it. Both are short lists of plain pages.

**Search settings.** While Settings is open the search box in the top bar says "Search settings" and finds pages by what they do (for example "undo", "backup", "mailbox"). Enter opens the first match, Escape clears it. Administrators' pages are only offered to administrators. Closing Settings brings back "Search inbox".

**On a phone** Settings opens as a list of big rows with a search box at the top; each page opens full screen with a Back button, and Back to inbox leaves Settings.

**Saving.** Forms save by themselves when you change something. Buttons that test a connection or store a key or password keep their button.

## How every page looks

Each page is a **list**. Rows sit together in rounded cards under a small grey heading. A row has its title on the left; on the right it may show a grey word (its state, such as "On" or "3 people"), a switch, a drop-down, or an arrow. An arrow means the row opens a page. That page replaces the list, with a blue **back link** above a big title, so you always know where you are and how to go back. The small **?** beside a title or a row opens the longer explanation, so the page itself stays calm. Red centred rows (Remove, Delete) never have an arrow and always ask first. It looks the same in dark and light.

**On a phone** each page opens full screen. The back link and title stay at the top and the buttons that finish a form (Save, Add) stay fixed at the bottom; only the middle scrolls.

## Settings (everyone): seven pages

| Page | What is on it |
| --- | --- |
| **Account** | Profile (name, email, about you, picture), Sign-in & security (password, two-factor, passkeys, recovery codes, signed-in devices), and Security and privacy. |
| **Mailboxes** | One row for each mailbox; open it for its switches, name, colour, Connection (Sync now, Check connection) and Diagnostics. **Add a mailbox**, and **Check all mailboxes now**. |
| **People & writing** | Address book, Important people, Signature & saved replies, and Your AI instructions (summary instructions, Writing help, My writing style). |
| **Rules & calendar** | Mail rules, Muted conversations, Away reply, and Calendar & to-do (calendar link, Google Calendar, instant calendar, Home Assistant list). |
| **Alerts** | Notifications (where they go and what to tell you about) and Email alerts. |
| **General** | Undo send, how long the Reviewed and Archived lists are kept, Features, Fast search, Your data, and Keyboard shortcuts. |
| **Help** | The setup guide. |

## Administration (administrators only): six pages

| Page | What is on it |
| --- | --- |
| **People & sign-in** | Sign-in rules, Single sign-on, the people (open one to change or remove them) and Add a person. |
| **Mail** | Automatic sync, Reviewed and Archived limits, Forward to check, and Microsoft sign-in. |
| **AI** | The AI model, Extra summary instructions and Agent chat. |
| **Features** | Switches for everything that can be turned off for everyone, in groups. |
| **Notifications** | Which places reminders can go, addresses on your own network, and Google Calendar. |
| **System** | Backup and restore, Updates, Reporting, and Security and privacy. |

Nothing was removed. Links and bookmarks to older names (for example `#/settings/calendar`) still open the right page. The stand-alone addresses `/account`, `/mailboxes` and `/admin` use the same lists (`/admin` opens People & sign-in).

## Saving

Simple choices save the moment you change them and show a quiet **Saved**. Pages that need several boxes filled in have a **Save** (or **Add**) row at the bottom of the page. Rows that test a connection or store a key or password keep their own row. Secrets are never shown again after they are saved.
