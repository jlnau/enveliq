The operator (a household member, an employer, a club) decides who gets an account and is responsible for how it is used. If you are the operator you should:

- Connect only mailboxes you own or have clear permission to use.
- Tell people what is processed and by whom. Employers should check the workplace surveillance and privacy rules that apply where they operate.
- Keep the server, its operating system and Enveliq up to date, and control who can reach it.
- Understand that if you switch on AI summaries with a cloud AI service, email text is sent to that company. Local AI servers keep it on your network.
